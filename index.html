Projects

Typing Task

A standardized user-testing task from logiTask for evaluating how well a keyboard supports typing. Participants type a passage while the screen marks their progress and flags mistakes, and they can correct errors with backspace or keep going. Performance is reported in words per minute (characters per minute for Simplified Chinese), which captures speed and errors together. Test duration, text, case sensitivity and “blocking” are all configurable, so results can be compared across keyboards and devices.
